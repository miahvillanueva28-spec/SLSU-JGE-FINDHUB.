Safe Claiming Guidelines

Your safety and security matter. Follow these steps to safely recover or return items at SLSU JGE Campus.

1 You Must Prove Ownership

"It looks like mine" is not enough. You must provide 3 or more of the following:

Proof Type Examples
🪪 School/Government ID Student ID, PhilSys ID — name must match exactly
📝 Unique Details Serial number, scratches, stickers, lock PIN, saved photos
📅 Exact Circumstances Time, exact location, who you were with — details only you would know
🧾 Purchase Proof Receipt, box photo, warranty, online order screenshot
👤 Witness Name & contact of someone who saw you with it

2 How the Claim Process Works

  1. Submit a Claim Request

    Go to the report page and fill in the verification form. Never share private info before verification.

  2. Reporter Reviews Your Details

    They compare your answers to what they know. No contact info shared yet.

  3. ✅ Match → Arrange Meeting

    Reporter shares contact & meeting details. ❌ No match → try again or provide more details.

  4. Meet & Confirm Handover

    Show your ID in person. Sign simple confirmation. Mark item as Returned in the system.

3 Safe Meeting Spots — SLSU JGE Only

✅ Safe Locations

  • Campus Security / Guard House
  • Student Center or Covered Court
  • Library Lobby or Admin Building
  • Your Department Office

❌ Never Go

  • Private rooms or empty areas
  • Off-campus locations
  • Meetings after dark
  • Alone — always bring a friend

🕐 Best Practice

Meet between 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM during weekdays. Bring 1 companion whenever possible.

! Red Flags — Stop & Report

🚩 These are NOT normal — report immediately

  • Asking for money, load, or payment to return an item
  • Pressuring you: "Someone else is coming — hurry!"
  • Refusing in-person meeting — insisting on shipping or photos only
  • Asking for passwords, PINs, or full ID photos before verification
  • Story changes or details don't match what was posted

📞 Report Concerns To

Campus Security / Admin Office — or message through this platform. We will review and take action.

✓ Quick Rules to Remember

✅ DO

  • Verify details first
  • Meet in public, well-lit areas
  • Bring your ID & a friend
  • Report suspicious behavior
  • Mark item as Returned

❌ DON'T

  • Never send money
  • Don't share info too early
  • Don't meet alone or at night
  • Don't rush — take your time
  • Don't hand over without ID check
